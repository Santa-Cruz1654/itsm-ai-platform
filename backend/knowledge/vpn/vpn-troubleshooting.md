# VPN Troubleshooting Guide

## Cannot connect

If the employee cannot connect to the corporate VPN, first verify that the internet connection is working.

### Verify internet access

Open a browser and verify that an external website can be reached.

### Restart the VPN client

Close the corporate VPN client completely and restart it.

### Reconnect

Enter the corporate credentials and reconnect.

## VPN authentication failure

If the VPN client reports an authentication failure, verify that the employee is using the current corporate credentials.

Do not share passwords with the service desk.

If the credentials are expired, use the approved password-reset workflow.

## Escalation

If the VPN still fails after these steps, collect the VPN client error message and escalate the incident to the Network Support team.
